# Notable Health

> Notable is the leading healthcare AI Platform purpose-built to transform workforce productivity across U.S. health systems, medical groups, and health plans. The company deploys AI Agents — configurable, end-to-end digital workers — that automate the administrative work behind patient care across Patient Access, Revenue Cycle Management, Care Operations, and the Contact Center. Founded in 2017 and headquartered in San Mateo, California, Notable is deployed at 12,000+ sites of care, has served 38M+ patients, and automates ~1.5M once-manual tasks daily.

Last updated: 2026-04-20

Notable is led by co-founders Pranay Kapadia (CEO), Adam Ting (CPO), and Justin White, PhD (CTO), with Aaron Neinstein, MD as Chief Medical Officer. The company is backed by ICONIQ Growth, Greylock Partners, F-Prime, Oak HC/FT, Maverick Ventures, and 8VC. Notable's stated mission is to simplify and optimize healthcare for humanity, with a goal of positively impacting 100 million patients.

**Product architecture.** The platform centers on three interrelated AI surfaces. **AI Agents** are autonomous or semi-autonomous digital workers that perform end-to-end workflows (e.g., submit a prior auth, close a care gap, handle an inbound call). **Sidekick** is an AI co-pilot for human staff — it synthesizes structured and unstructured data, cites sources, generates clinical packets for review, and keeps a human in the loop on complex cases (canonical example: complex prior authorization submissions). **Flow AI** is a natural-language assistant inside **Flow Builder** — Notable's low-code drag-and-drop interface for designing, testing, deploying, and monitoring automations — that helps builders (IT, ops, clinical leaders) design, debug, and optimize workflows conversationally, reducing reliance on vendor-led development cycles.

**Key facts for interpreting this file:**

- Notable is B2B: customers are healthcare provider organizations (health systems, hospitals, medical groups, ambulatory surgery centers, urgent care networks, FQHCs) and health plans — not individual patients. Patients experience Notable indirectly through the workflows it powers (online scheduling, digital intake, appointment reminders, Voice/SMS outreach, patient-facing Assistant), but Notable does not sell to consumers.
- The platform is horizontal and configurable, not a point solution. The same underlying AI Platform supports registration, scheduling, referrals, authorizations, chart review, care gap closure, contact center automation, and revenue cycle — customers can start with one workflow and expand. Many customers replace several legacy point solutions with Notable.
- Integration depth is core to the product. Notable reads and writes bidirectionally in real time across EHRs (Epic, Oracle Health / Cerner, MEDITECH, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks), payer portals, HIEs, CRMs, claims platforms, and data warehouses — using a combination of FHIR APIs, HL7 interfaces, RPA ("digital assistants" that click and type in the EHR like a human would), and third-party APIs. The **Connector Hub**, embedded in Flow Builder, gives builders a single view of what systems and data domains are already connected. The EHR remains the source of truth.
- The four marketed solution pillars on the site are **Patient Access**, **Revenue Cycle Management**, **Care Operations** (also called Value-Based Care), and **Contact Center**. Older/alternate URLs use labels like "Growth, Acquisition, Access" and "Value-Based Care" — these are the same concepts.
- Notable is enterprise-grade secure. The company is SOC 2 Type II audited, ISO 27001:2022 certified, and HIPAA compliant; customers can verify via the Trust Center at trust.notablehealth.com.
- Named customers include Intermountain Health, MUSC Health, CommonSpirit Health, UC San Diego Health, Montage Health, Presbyterian, CityMD, Gillette Children's, Optum, Catholic Health, Community Health Network, Marshfield Clinic Health System and Security Health Plan, North Kansas City Hospital, Meritas Health, Austin Regional Clinic, Castell, Fort HealthCare, Care New England, Imperial Valley Healthcare District, San Antonio Regional Hospital, MIT Health, Beacon Health System, Regional One Health, and Marshall Health Network.
- Headquarters: 101 S Ellsworth Ave, San Mateo, CA 94401. General inquiries: hello@notablehealth.com. The primary conversion action across the site is "Request a demo."

Sidekick uses natural language and deep document analysis to support complex, clinical-reasoning-heavy workflows (most notably complex prior authorization submissions). Notable connects to Epic, Oracle Health (Cerner), MEDITECH, athenahealth, and other EHRs, plus payer portals, HIEs, CRMs, claims platforms, and data warehouses — using FHIR APIs, HL7 interfaces, RPA, and third-party APIs.

## Use cases (solution pillars)

- [Patient Access](https://www.notablehealth.com/use-cases/access): Automate fax intake, referrals, self-scheduling, digital registration and pre-visit intake, appointment reminders, and prescription-pickup flows to remove access barriers, shorten waits, and prevent no-shows. Improves upfront data capture to reduce downstream denials.
- [Revenue Cycle Management](https://www.notablehealth.com/use-case/revenue-cycle-management): Automate eligibility checks, prior authorizations, coding, denial management, appeals, copay estimation and collection, and NOA fax submissions — to prevent denials, reduce cost-to-collect, speed reimbursement, and secure revenue without adding headcount. Average ~$2.8M annual value from automating RCM tasks per organization cited in Notable's analysis.
- [Care Operations / Value-Based Care](https://www.notablehealth.com/use-case/value-based-care): Close care gaps, automate HCC chart reviews, optimize risk adjustment, track open quality measures for ACO/MA contracts, and coordinate post-discharge follow-ups — to raise Star ratings, improve outcomes, and earn shared savings.
- [Contact Center](https://www.notablehealth.com/use-case/contact-center): Automate inbound and outbound patient communication with conversational AI-powered voice and SMS Agents. Handles scheduling, FAQs, prior-auth statusing, medication refills, nurse triage, and multi-intent conversations end-to-end — replacing rigid IVRs with natural-language understanding that integrates directly with the EHR.

## Core product solutions

- [Registration & Intake](https://www.notablehealth.com/solutions/intelligent-intake): Replace clipboards and paper forms with an AI-powered digital check-in that pre-populates data, scans insurance cards, captures consents, and writes back to the EHR — reducing check-in times and registration staff burden.
- [Scheduling & Referrals](https://www.notablehealth.com/solutions/intelligent-scheduling): Let patients digitally schedule the right appointment with the right provider at the right time, without phone calls. Reduce no-shows and late cancellations through intelligent reminders and self-service rescheduling.
- [Authorizations](https://www.notablehealth.com/solutions/intelligent-authorizations): Automate prior authorization submission, statusing, work-queue cleanup, appeals management, and authorization verification. Routine submissions run touchless; complex clinical cases are handled by Sidekick with human review.
- [Referrals](https://www.notablehealth.com/solutions/intelligent-referrals): OCR faxed referrals, extract structured and unstructured data, triage, notify specialists, and schedule patients — all with bi-directional EHR integration. Turnaround typically 10-15 minutes.
- [Care Gap Closure](https://www.notablehealth.com/solutions/care-gap-closure): AI Agents identify and verify open care gaps (mammograms, wellness visits, screenings), scrub both structured and unstructured chart data to validate status, send SMS outreach to schedule, and write evidence back to the EHR — improving quality scores with no staff intervention.
- [HCC Chart Review](https://www.notablehealth.com/solutions/hcc-chart-review): AI extracts diagnoses from unstructured EHR data and claims to surface provider-trusted HCC suspects. Uses up-to-date v28 mappings to drive accurate point-of-care documentation while keeping providers in their native EHR workflow.
- [Assistant](https://www.notablehealth.com/solutions/assistant): A patient-facing conversational AI that handles scheduling, bill pay, navigation, medication refills, and FAQs. Supports 150 languages via voice or text, deflects calls from the contact center, and augments web, portal, and mobile channels.
